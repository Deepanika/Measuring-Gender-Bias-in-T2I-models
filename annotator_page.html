Before you start

What you will do

You will look at AI-generated images of everyday activities in India. For each image you mark every person, say what role they have in the scene using a list of roles we give you, and record their perceived gender from appearance. Most images take about five minutes. You can stop at any time and continue later in the same browser.

What we store

  • Your name and the time you gave consent. These are kept in a separate private file that only the researcher sees, and are never published.
  • Your coder ID, your answers for each image, how long you spent on each image, and any image you flag for discussion.
  • If you choose to give them, your gender and age band, used only to describe the group of coders as a whole.

How your answers are used

Your answers will be compared with answers from automated systems, to check how accurate those systems are, and with other coders' answers. Results appear in a course report. Your answers will be published in a public research repository under your coder ID only, without your name.

Taking part is voluntary

You can stop at any time without giving a reason. Until the final report is submitted, you can ask for your answers to be deleted by writing to . Some images show home scenes, prayer, sleep or caregiving; none are graphic.

New images for you

The images you already finished are kept and will not be shown as new work. Everything else in the study information you agreed to stays the same.

Your earlier answers are saved. If you would rather not code more images, just tell the researcher; nothing else is needed.
